Our Story

Built from a
Conversation

“We weren’t trying to build a company. We were just two students complaining about a problem every student around us had.”

It started the way most real things do — not with a business plan or a pitch deck, but with frustration. In 2025, two students at Bells University of Technology in Ota, Ogun State, were having the kind of conversation that happens in every hostel across Nigeria. Why are the things we need so expensive here? Why does nobody seem to be building this properly for students?

One of them — studying Civil Engineering — had always been entrepreneurial, always seeing opportunity where others saw problems. The other — a software developer studying Mechanical Engineering — had the technical ability to actually build something from scratch. Between the two of them, they had exactly what it takes to go from an idea to reality.

2025 was the year of thoughts. Of fantasising in the way that students do — sketching ideas on phones, arguing about what it should be, shelving it, coming back to it. Most conversations like this go nowhere. This one refused to die.

2026 was the year they stopped talking and started building. Reavyve went from a hostel conversation to a fully functional store — every product category, every checkout flow, every order confirmation — designed and developed by two students who understood exactly what it should feel like, because they were living it themselves.

They didn’t wait to graduate. They didn’t wait for the perfect moment. They built it now, because Nigerian students needed it now. That’s Reavyve.

Common Questions

Everything You
Want to Know

Can’t find your answer? Email us and we’ll get back to you quickly.

How do I place an order?▼
Browse the store, add items to your cart, then tap Checkout. You’ll see our bank account details automatically. Transfer the total, upload your payment receipt, and tap “I Have Paid.” We receive your order and confirm it via email.
How long does delivery take?▼
Most orders are delivered within 24 hours to campus addresses. For orders placed late at night, delivery happens the next morning. We always communicate your delivery time via email after confirming payment.
What payment methods do you accept?▼
We currently accept direct bank transfers. Our account details are shown automatically at checkout. After transferring, upload your payment receipt through the store and we’ll confirm your order.
Are your products genuine?▼
Absolutely. Every product on Reavyve is sourced from verified suppliers and inspected before listing. We only sell brand-new, authentic items. If you receive something that doesn’t match what was advertised, contact us immediately and we will resolve it.
Do you offer returns or refunds?▼
We don’t have a standard returns policy because every product is quality-verified before dispatch. However, if you receive something damaged, incorrect, or not as described, email us at reavyvestores@gmail.com immediately and we will make it right at no cost to you.
Do I need an account to shop?▼
You can browse the full store without an account. To use the wishlist or proceed to checkout, you’ll need a free account. Registration takes under a minute — just your name, email, and a password.
What is a Flash Sale?▼
Flash Sales are time-limited deals where selected products are offered at heavily discounted prices — sometimes up to 40% off. They appear with a live countdown timer at the top of the store. Once the timer ends, the sale ends. Check regularly so you don’t miss them.
Which campuses do you deliver to?▼
Reavyve is currently live at Bells University of Technology, Ota. We are actively working to expand to more campuses across Nigeria. If you’re at a different school and want Reavyve there, send us an email — your campus could be next.
How do I track my order?▼
After confirming your payment, we send order updates to your email — when packed, when dispatched, and when arrived. Make sure to check your inbox.
How do I contact support?▼
Email us at reavyvestores@gmail.com. We aim to respond within a few hours during business hours — 8am to 8pm WAT, Monday to Saturday.
Can I cancel an order after paying?▼
You can request cancellation before your order is dispatched. Email us at reavyvestores@gmail.com as soon as possible with your Order ID. Once dispatched, cancellation may not be possible but we will do our best to help.
Is my information safe?▼
Yes. We never store card details — payments are bank transfers only. Your receipt image is used solely for order verification. Personal data is stored securely in Google Firebase and handled per our Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account on Reavyve, we collect your name, email address, and password. When you place an order, we collect your order details, campus address, and payment receipt image. We do not collect or store your bank card details or banking credentials.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide exclusively to process your orders, confirm payments, facilitate delivery, and communicate with you about your purchases. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Payment Information

Reavyve uses direct bank transfer as its payment method. We do not process card payments and do not collect or store payment card information. Your payment receipt images are uploaded to secure third-party image hosting and used solely for order verification.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your account data is stored securely in Google Firebase, a cloud platform compliant with industry security standards. We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

5. Your Rights

  • You may request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us at reavyvestores@gmail.com
  • You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • You may correct inaccurate information in your account profile

6. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please contact us at reavyvestores@gmail.com.
