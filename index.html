Common Questions

Everything You
Want to Know

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply within minutes.

How do I place an order on Reavyve?▾
Browse the store, add items to your cart, then tap Checkout. You'll see our bank account details automatically. Transfer the total amount, upload your payment receipt, and tap "I Have Paid." We receive your order on WhatsApp and confirm it within minutes.
How long does delivery take?▾
Most orders are delivered within 24 hours to campus addresses. For orders placed late at night, delivery happens the next morning. We will always communicate your delivery time via WhatsApp after confirming your payment.
What payment methods do you accept?▾
We currently accept direct bank transfers only. Our account details (bank name, account number, and account name) are shown automatically when you proceed to checkout. After transferring, upload your payment receipt through the store and we'll confirm your order.
Are your products genuine and authentic?▾
Absolutely. Every product on Reavyve is sourced from verified suppliers and inspected before listing. We only sell brand-new, authentic items. If you receive something that doesn't match what was advertised, contact us immediately and we will resolve it.
Do you offer returns or refunds?▾
We don't have a standard returns policy because we don't believe you'll need one — every product is quality-verified before dispatch. However, if you receive a product that is damaged, incorrect, or not as described, contact us on WhatsApp immediately and we will make it right at no cost to you.
Do I need an account to shop?▾
You can browse the entire store without an account. However, to use the wishlist feature and to proceed to checkout, you'll need to create a free account. Registration takes less than 30 seconds — just your name, email, and a password.
What is a Flash Sale?▾
Flash Sales are time-limited deals where selected products are offered at heavily discounted prices — sometimes up to 40% off. They appear as a countdown timer at the top of the store. Once the timer runs out, the sale ends and normal pricing resumes. Check the store regularly to catch them.
Which campuses do you deliver to?▾
We currently serve campuses nationwide in Nigeria. When you proceed to checkout and send your order via WhatsApp, include your campus address and we will confirm if delivery is available in your area. We are constantly expanding our delivery coverage.
How do I track my order?▾
All order tracking happens via WhatsApp. After we confirm your payment, we send you updates through the same chat — when your order is packed, when it's dispatched, and when it arrives. Save our WhatsApp number so you don't miss updates.
How do I contact customer support?▾
The fastest way is WhatsApp — tap the WhatsApp icon in the store's footer or contact page. You can also email us at [email protected]. We aim to respond to all messages within 1 hour during business hours (8am – 8pm WAT, Monday to Saturday).
Can I cancel an order after paying?▾
You can request a cancellation before your order is dispatched. Message us on WhatsApp as soon as possible with your Order ID. Once the order has left for delivery, cancellation may not be possible but we will do our best to accommodate you.
Is my payment information safe?▾
Yes. Reavyve uses direct bank transfer — we never store your banking credentials or card details. Your payment receipt image is uploaded securely and used only for order verification. Your personal information is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account on Reavyve, we collect your name, email address, and password. When you place an order, we collect your order details, campus address, and payment receipt image. We do not collect or store your bank card details or banking credentials.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide exclusively to process your orders, confirm payments, facilitate delivery, and communicate with you about your purchases. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Payment Information

Reavyve uses direct bank transfer as its payment method. We do not process card payments and therefore do not collect or store payment card information. Your payment receipt images are uploaded to secure third-party image hosting and used solely for order verification.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your account data is stored securely in Google Firebase, a cloud platform compliant with industry security standards. We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

5. Cookies & Analytics

The Reavyve store uses browser localStorage and sessionStorage to cache product data and improve loading speed. We do not currently use third-party analytics or advertising cookies. Your shopping session data remains on your own device.

  • You may correct inaccurate information in your account profile
